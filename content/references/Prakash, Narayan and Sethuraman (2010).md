
In the study , the authors explore student perceptions of the usefulness of explicitly discussing the "Structure of the Observed Learning Outcome" (SOLO) taxonomy. The SOLO taxonomy is a model that classifies learning outcomes from simple to complex. The study involved a group of students in a physiology course who were exposed to the SOLO taxonomy's framework as a means of enhancing their learning and understanding. The students were then surveyed to gauge their perception of the explicit instruction on the SOLO taxonomy. The findings indicated that the majority of students found the explicit discussion of the SOLO taxonomy to be useful for their learning process, suggesting that students can benefit from an understanding of educational frameworks that structure their learning outcomes.
